**Date:** 2026-05-21

## 🎯 Objective Verification
The objective of Milestone v10.0 was to successfully simplify and restructure the `auto-action` automation module by removing legacy triggers, rule templates, and tightly-coupled pipelines, and instead introducing fully isolated, independent `actionEngine` and `conditionEngine` systems. This has been completely achieved:
1. **Coupling Removal**: Legacy files `evaluator.ts`, `registry.ts`, `template.ts`, and `scopes/task/triggers.ts` were deleted. Triggers are completely removed from registration and execution.
2. **Decoupled engines**: Standalone `conditionEngine.ts` handles stateless AST evaluation, and standalone `actionEngine.ts` handles Zod-validated dynamic execution.
3. **Optimistic Concurrency Control**: The `setFields` action handles updates safely in high-concurrency settings via standard optimistic locking and row-count verification.
