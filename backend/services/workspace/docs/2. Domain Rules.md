# Domain Rules: Project & Team

This document outlines the strict business and structural rules enforced within the Taskinator Workspace service.

## 1. Project Rules

### Ownership & Membership
*   **The "Strict Owner" Rule:** The project owner is the creator and ultimate authority. They are **not** automatically added to the `ProjectMembers` table. They exist in the `Projects` table as the `ownerId`.
*   **Member Isolation:** A user must be explicitly added to a project's members list to be considered a participant.
*   **Ownership Security:** Only the project owner is authorized to add or remove members.

### Uniqueness & Integrity
*   **Name Uniqueness:** A user cannot have two projects with the exact same name. Names are unique per `ownerId`.
*   **Concurrent Safety:** All project updates and deletions must pass an **Optimistic Locking** check using the `version` column.

---

## 2. Team Rules

### Membership Hierarchy
*   **The "Parent Project" Restriction:** Only users who are either **Project Members** or the **Project Owner** are allowed to join a team within that project.
*   **Team Membership Isolation:** Being a project member does not automatically make you a team member. Membership must be assigned per team.

### Structural Integrity (The Hierarchy)
*   **The "One Parent" Rule:** A team can have at most one parent. To maintain a strict tree structure and prevent complex graph cycles, a team cannot be re-parented if it already has an existing parent ID.
*   **Depth Guard:** Team hierarchies are capped at a maximum depth of **50 levels**. Any attempt to create a team deeper than this will be rejected to prevent database performance degradation.
*   **Name Uniqueness:** Team names must be unique within their parent project.

### Persistence & Deletion
*   **Optimistic Locking:** Just like projects, team updates require a matching `version` number.
*   **Cascade Cleanup:** When a project is deleted, all associated teams and hierarchy entries are cleaned up asynchronously.
