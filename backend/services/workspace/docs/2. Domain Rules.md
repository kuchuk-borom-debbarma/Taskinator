# Domain Rules: Project & Team

This document outlines the strict business and structural rules enforced within the Taskinator Workspace service.

## 1. Project Rules

### Ownership & Membership
*   **The "Strict Owner" Rule:** The project owner is the creator and ultimate authority. They exist in the `Projects` table as the `ownerId`. 
*   **Membership Isolation:** Owners are **not** automatically added to the `ProjectMembers` table. They are authorized by virtue of their ownership record.
*   **Security Guard:** Only the project owner is authorized to add members to a project or modify its core details.

### Uniqueness & Integrity
*   **Name Uniqueness:** A user cannot have two projects with the exact same name. Names are unique per `ownerId`.
*   **Concurrent Safety:** All project updates and deletions must pass an **Optimistic Locking** check using the `version` column.

---

## 2. Team Rules

### Membership & "Suicide Safety"
*   **The "Project Container" Rule:** Only users who are explicit **Project Members** are allowed to join a team within that project.
*   **Owner Exception:** The **Project Owner** is always permitted to join any team, regardless of their status in the `ProjectMembers` table.

### Structural Integrity (The Hierarchy)
*   **The "One Parent" Rule:** A team can have at most one parent. 
    *   **Immutability:** Once a team is linked to a parent, it cannot be re-parented to a different branch. 
    *   **Root-to-Leaf Only:** Only "Root" teams (those without a parent) can be linked to an existing hierarchy.
*   **Depth Guard:** Team hierarchies are capped at a maximum depth of **50 levels**.
*   **Name Uniqueness:** Team names must be unique within their parent project context.

### Persistence & Deletion
*   **Optimistic Deletion:** Team deletions require a matching `version` check to prevent deleting a record that was modified by another user concurrently.
*   **Atomic Cleanup:** If a team deletion succeeds, all its hierarchy entries in the Closure Table are removed instantly.
